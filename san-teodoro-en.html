A Corner of Sardinia to Discover

San Teodoro lies on the north-eastern coast of Sardinia, in an area where the sea meets Mediterranean scrubland, lagoons and the beautiful landscapes of the inland region.

The town is an ideal starting point for enjoying days by the sea, walks in nature and evenings exploring the town centre, with shops, bars and restaurants that come alive especially during the summer.

Every day can offer a different experience: a morning on the beach, an excursion along the coast, or an evening discovering local flavours and traditions.

Beaches You Shouldn't Miss

Pale sand, crystal-clear waters and ever-changing scenery: the beaches are among San Teodoro's greatest treasures.

La Cinta

La Cinta is San Teodoro's iconic beach: a long stretch of fine, pale sand washed by clear waters, with sandy seabeds that slope gently into the sea. Behind the beach lies the lagoon, while on clear days you can see Tavolara Island. La Cinta is perfect for strolling along the shoreline, enjoying a family beach day or taking part in water activities where permitted. The beach has bars, refreshment points, beach clubs with sunbed and equipment rentals, and visitor facilities. Parking is available near the entrances and is generally paid during the summer season. During busy periods, arriving early is recommended. Check access conditions and available services in advance.

Cala Brandinchi

Also known as “Little Tahiti”, Cala Brandinchi is famous for its pale sand, turquoise waters and shallow sandy seabed. Its scenery resembles a tropical lagoon, with beautiful views towards Tavolara Island. It is popular with families and anyone looking to swim and relax in a natural setting. During busy periods, it is important to plan your visit in advance. Summer access may be regulated and reservations may be required. Before setting off, check the latest rules, availability and access arrangements on the official San Teodoro tourism website.

Lu Impostu

Lu Impostu is a wide, sunlit beach with fine, pale sand, crystal-clear water and Mediterranean vegetation. Its natural scenery makes it an ideal destination for relaxing by the sea and enjoying the colours of the San Teodoro coastline. The sandy seabed and open views are especially beautiful on sunny days. In summer, the beach can be very busy and access may be subject to reservations or other specific rules. Before visiting, check the latest regulations, access arrangements and parking information on the official San Teodoro tourism website.

L'Isuledda

L'Isuledda is a pale-sand beach surrounded by Mediterranean vegetation and overlooking a sea with beautiful turquoise hues. From here, you can enjoy lovely views of Tavolara Island and the surrounding coastline. It is a great choice for spending a few hours by the sea, relaxing on the sand or exploring the water with a mask and snorkel when conditions allow. The area can be very busy during the summer months. Before visiting, check sea conditions, available services and any access or parking restrictions.

Cala d'Ambra

Cala d'Ambra is close to San Teodoro town centre, making it a convenient choice for anyone who wants to spend a few hours by the sea without travelling far from town. The beach has pale sand and a pleasant atmosphere, ideal for combining relaxation by the water with a stroll through the town centre. Its location makes it easy to plan a flexible day, perhaps stopping at a café or restaurant before or after the beach. Before visiting, check sea conditions and the latest information about services and parking options.

Capo Coda Cavallo

Capo Coda Cavallo is one of the most scenic coastal areas near San Teodoro, with headlands, small bays and spectacular views of Tavolara Island and the Tavolara–Punta Coda Cavallo Marine Protected Area. It is ideal for exploring the coastline, admiring the scenery and discovering coves with crystal-clear water. In suitable conditions, some areas are also great for snorkelling, always respecting the rules of the protected area. Before setting off, check sea conditions, parking options and any access restrictions, which may vary by season and location.

A tip before you go: Access to Cala Brandinchi and Lu Impostu may be regulated and reservations may be required, especially in summer. Always check the latest rules on the official San Teodoro tourism website.

Things to Do in San Teodoro

A holiday is about more than just the sea: the surrounding area offers many ways to discover Sardinia and enjoy different experiences.

Boat Trips

Discover the coastline from the sea, admire Tavolara and Molara, and consider joining an organised excursion in the marine protected area with an authorised operator.

Snorkelling and Water Sports

When conditions are suitable, explore the seabed in permitted areas or try activities such as kayaking, sailing and other water sports offered by local operators.

Nature and Walks

Take some time to walk along the coast, admire the lagoon landscapes and discover the Mediterranean vegetation. In wetland areas, observe birds without disturbing wildlife.

The Town Centre and Summer Evenings

Stroll through the town's streets, browse the shops, stop for an aperitif or choose a restaurant to enjoy local cuisine. In summer, the town centre comes alive with markets and seasonal events.

The Flavours of Sardinia

Make the most of your holiday by discovering local specialities, from traditional dishes and regional products to Sardinian wines.

A Relaxed Day at Your Own Pace

There's no need to plan every moment: choose a beach, enjoy the scenery and leave time for a stop at a café or a walk at sunset.

Discover the Surrounding Area

Tavolara and Molara

Just off the coast lie the islands of Tavolara and Molara, offering some of the most breathtaking views in the area. A boat trip is a wonderful way to admire the sea from a different perspective and discover the beauty of the marine protected area while respecting local regulations.

Monte Nieddu and the Inland Landscapes

If you would like to combine beach time with nature, the inland area offers green landscapes, hills and panoramic views. Monte Nieddu is worth exploring along routes and trails suited to your experience. Always check trail conditions and accessibility beforehand.

Porto San Paolo

A short distance from San Teodoro, Porto San Paolo is a coastal destination with beautiful views of Tavolara and opportunities for boat trips. It is a great choice for a day out that is different from a typical beach day.

Budoni and Its Coastline

Nearby Budoni offers beaches, Mediterranean vegetation and a charming town centre. It is a pleasant destination for a walk or for spending a few hours discovering another part of this stretch of coastline.

Olbia and the Historic Centre

For a day of shopping, squares and restaurants, head to Olbia and explore its historic centre. It is an interesting alternative to a day at the beach, especially if you would like to discover another side of north-eastern Sardinia.

Coastal Destinations

Every stretch of coastline has its own character, from small bays and wide beaches to scenic viewpoints. Plan a day trip according to the time you have available, sea conditions and local advice.

Useful Tips for Your Holiday

To make the most of San Teodoro and its surroundings, a few simple tips can help make every day easier and more enjoyable.

Plan Your Beach Day

During the summer months, the most popular beaches can get very busy. Before setting off, check any access rules, reservation requirements and parking information.

Check the Weather and Sea Conditions

Wind and sea conditions can affect your plans, especially if you are considering a boat trip or water sports. Check the latest forecasts and always follow local advice.

Respect Nature

Beaches, dunes and lagoons are precious natural environments. Stay on permitted paths, take your rubbish with you and avoid disturbing wildlife. Small actions help preserve the beauty of this area.
