BougainVilla

Holiday Home in San Teodoro

BougainVilla is located in San Teodoro, between Gallura and Baronia, in the heart of north-eastern Sardinia. Nestled at the foot of Monte Nieddu, it is the ideal starting point for discovering the beaches, nature and culture of this beautiful corner of Sardinia.

Discover BougainVilla

Welcome to BougainVilla

Your space, your holiday

BougainVilla is an independent apartment where you can enjoy your holiday in complete freedom, with welcoming interiors and outdoor spaces to enjoy at any time of day.

The garden, hammock, terrace with its outdoor lounge area and barbecue are perfect for breakfast outside, a relaxing break after a day at the beach or an evening with friends.

The fully equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi and air conditioning complete the amenities designed for your stay.

Beaches, Nature and Local Life

Located at the foot of Monte Nieddu, between Gallura and Baronia, BougainVilla is the ideal starting point for discovering the beaches, landscapes and traditions of north-eastern Sardinia.

The centre of San Teodoro offers lovely opportunities for walks, restaurants, cafés and lively summer evenings. Close to the house, you will also find a supermarket, a playground open during the summer season and a free outdoor fitness area.

Perfect for Families, Couples and Friends

BougainVilla offers an independent space to share with your loved ones, while giving you the freedom to plan each day at your own pace.

Families can enjoy the outdoor spaces, couples can find moments of peace and quiet, and groups of friends can explore San Teodoro's evening life.

After a day out, the garden and terrace are perfect for getting together and enjoying a little more of the day.

Information and Bookings

San Teodoro and La Cinta

San Teodoro is one of Sardinia's most popular destinations, famous for its crystal-clear sea, white sandy beaches and unspoilt nature.

One of the area's highlights is La Cinta, one of the best-known and most beautiful beaches in the region.

During your stay, you can discover the sea, explore San Teodoro town centre, enjoy its restaurants and bars, and discover the area's many natural attractions.
